# Source Verification for Printed Map Claims

Use this reference when deciding whether a research finding is strong enough to place on a finished map.

| Printed claim | Useful evidence | If evidence is missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outdoor path or crossing | Current official site map or mapped geometry checked against recent imagery | Omit the connection; retain independent anchored spots |
| Room adjacency and doors | Current visitor floor plan or supplied venue plan | Label the sheet schematic; do not invent a route |
| Floor name and transfer | Venue plan identifying floor and stair/lift | Retain source labels and identify the unresolved transfer |
| Public access and photography gear | Current venue policy applying to the proposed session | State the specific open constraint; do not imply permission |
| Exact sunset time or cardinal lighting | Supplied date, local timezone, and verified sun position | Describe useful relative light conditions |
| Window-light direction | Verified window location/orientation and relevant timing | Make the setup conditional |
| Source attribution | The map or plan provider's attribution requirements | Preserve the source credit and links |

Keep a compact working record: source URL or supplied file, what it establishes, applicable date/floor/zone, and unresolved claims. Map pins, notes, and route arrows must agree with that record and the shared stop list.

The bundled style image establishes appearance only. Its room/landmark labels, compass, exposure settings, and timing do not establish facts for a new map. See [rendering.md](rendering.md) for the final visual checks.
